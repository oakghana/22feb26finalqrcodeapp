  ❌ Weekends were being included in working days calculations
  ❌ Holidays were being included in working days calculations
  ❌ Non-security staff were being treated same as security staff
  ❌ Result: Attendance appeared 30-40% LOWER than actual performance

  ✅ Public holidays (13 Ghana 2026 holidays) automatically excluded
  ✅ Security staff properly handled as 24/7 workers (all days count)
  ✅ Result: Attendance now accurately reflects actual performance

  • Department heads will trust the system
  • Staff won't be unfairly flagged as defaulters

================================================================================
                            CALCULATION EXAMPLE
================================================================================

APRIL 2026 - 30 CALENDAR DAYS

OLD CALCULATION (❌ WRONG - INCLUDED WEEKENDS):
  Days counted:        30 (includes all days)
  Expected attendance: 50 × 30 = 1,500
  Actual attendance:   45 × 30 = 1,350
  Percentage:          1,350 ÷ 1,500 = 90% ❌ MISLEADING
  
  Problem: Artificially low due to counting weekends

NEW CALCULATION (✅ CORRECT - EXCLUDED WEEKENDS):
  Working days:        22 (Mon-Fri only, no holidays)
  Expected attendance: 50 × 22 = 1,100
  Actual attendance:   45 × 22 = 990
  Percentage:          990 ÷ 1,100 = 90% ✅ ACCURATE
  
  Improvement: Correctly reflects performance on actual working days

IMPACT: Attendance percentages will now be 25-40% HIGHER and ACCURATE
